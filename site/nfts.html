The Score is the maze, as far as you got.

Every run mints the day's real maze as a small floating model. The art never gives the maze away: while the day is live, every Score is sealed to the entrance corner. After 00:00 UTC the maze is retired forever and the Score develops, raising a round patch of walls as far as your run got. A radius, never a route.

Today, sealed

Six runs of today's maze. Only the corner every player sees at spawn is drawn. The figure shows where each run ended: beside the monolith if found, or on the straight line from START toward the centre, as far in as it got. No walls, no path, nothing to copy.

The same runs, developed

What those six Scores turn into once the day ends. More walls rise for runs that got further, but they rise in a circle around the entrance, so even a finished run shows a patch of the maze, not the way through it. Mazes never repeat, so a retired one gives nothing away.

Five rooms

Each day draws one of five rooms from the portfolio: the red room, dusk, the pool, iris and the dunes. These are past days, already developed.

Power-Ups are guns

Everyone starts with the K-7. A Power-Up is a gun you keep: it trades raw damage for one move, with a few charges per run, and each one acts its move out on a plinth of maze concrete. Every HAM gun carries the same sight, a sliver of the orange monolith. Guns are sold as sealed packs, 10,000 in all. Every pack costs the same: the day's first is about $1, and each one sold makes the next dearer, ten times dearer every 100 packs. Back to $1 at midnight UTC. Strip one to see which gun is inside.

What draws it

Nothing here is stored as an image. tokenURI() rebuilds the drawing from fields the contract already records.

day
Seeds the maze, with the same generator the game uses, and picks the room.
day + block time
Sealed while the day is live, developed after 00:00 UTC. No one has to update anything: tokenURI() checks the clock.
completed
Lights the monolith and puts the figure beside it, or dims the room.
timeMs + kills
On a lost run, how far in the figure stands and how wide the developed patch is: about a cell per 20 seconds survived, plus half a cell per kill.
gun + runs
Which Power-Up it is, and how many runs it has been carried on.
points
The number on the card. Ranking uses the same figure.
