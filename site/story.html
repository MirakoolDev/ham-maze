How it works

Each run is a choice.

How it works once it's live on Robinhood Chain. A run costs the entry fee, and losing costs the same. There are no free retries, and your first run of the day counts most: everyone gets the same maze, so each run after it keeps 8% fewer points, down to 60%. Nothing happens on its own. You decide when to play, when to sell and when to claim.

The Score

The maze, as far as you got.

Every Score draws the day's real maze, built on chain from the date with the same generator the game uses. The art never gives the maze away. While the day is live, every Score is sealed to the entrance corner everyone sees at spawn. After 00:00 UTC that maze is retired for good and the Score develops: walls rise in a circle around the entrance, as far as your run got. A radius, never a route.

Built on the pot

What grows on top of it.

Two things reward people for staying and for bringing friends. A third is something HAM chose not to make.

01 · Crew bonus

Bring three, double the cut

A referrer earns 5% of each referred player's prize. Once three of them play on the same day, it becomes 10% for the rest of that day.

02 · Power-Ups

Guns you keep

Everyone starts with the K-7. A Power-Up is a gun NFT that trades raw damage for one move, with a few charges per run. Every gun comes in a sealed pack at one price: the day's first is about $1, and it goes ten times dearer every 100 packs sold. Back to $1 at midnight UTC. 10,000 packs. Strip one to see what you got.

03 · No new token

The pot buys $PFWA

HAM doesn't launch a coin. Its treasury buys $PFWA, the one token behind everything this builder ships, starting with a prediction market. Every new project feeds the same token.

Is this money, or just points?

This list is checked against the contract, not the pitch. Items that are still open say so.

No new token

Holds

No HAM coin exists. The treasury's share buys $PFWA.

01

Results can't be forged

Holds

The referee signs each result for one player, one attempt and one day. A changed score or a reused signature fails.

02

No double-dip on a sale

Holds

Starting a claim freezes the Score. A sale either completes first, and the buyer claims, or it fails.

03

Prizes don't disappear

Holds

A prize left unclaimed for 7 days goes back into the pot, not to the owner.

04

What the owner can do

Partly

The owner can pause new runs, set the fee up to 1 ETH and tune the maze within fixed limits. It can't move prizes or edit a signed result.

05

One referee key

Partly

A single key signs results. If it leaked, fake runs could be signed until it's replaced.

06

106 contract tests pass. There is no mainnet deployment or outside audit yet.

07

Glossary

Plain words.

Run
One paid attempt at the day's maze. It pays the fee, records the signed result and mints a Score, all in one transaction.
Score
The NFT a run becomes: the day's maze drawn as far as you got, with points, time, kills and whether you found the monolith.
Monolith
The orange glass shaft at the centre of every maze. Reaching it finishes the run.
Settle
Fixes the day's top three Scores and their prizes, 15 minutes after the day ends.
Claim intent
The first half of a claim. It freezes the Score so it can't change hands.
Collect
The second half, ten minutes later. It pays whoever holds the frozen Score.
Crew
The players one person referred who played that day. Three of them doubles the referral cut.
Power-Up
A Power-Up NFT you keep: Marker, Freeze, Pulse, Blinker or Snare. Each swaps raw damage for one move with a few charges per run.
Crate
A one-use pickup found inside a run: Blink, Freeze, Steal, Sonar, Shield or Decoy.
Sealed
How every Score looks while its day is live: only the entrance corner, so the art never helps anyone solve today's maze.
$PFWA
One token behind everything this builder ships. It began with a prediction market; HAM is the latest to plug in. The letters mean nothing. That's the point. HAM has no coin of its own: its pot buys $PFWA.
