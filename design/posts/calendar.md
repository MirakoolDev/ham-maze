# HAM daily post calendar

One post a day on X. The daily task reads the line for today's date, makes the card
in posts.html, renders it, and writes the post text to design/posts/out/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
Edit this file to change what goes out. Dates are local.

Facts every card must keep straight (from site/story.html and the contracts):
- One maze a day, the same for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. The orange monolith at its centre is home.
- Every run is a Score NFT you keep. The day's pot splits 40 / 20 / 10 to the top three Scores,
  10% to backers (for six months), 10% to holder bonuses and referrals, then the $PFWA buyback, 10% into tomorrow's pot.
- Backer Pass on FundingWorks: 0.05 ETH, mint closes Dec 5 2026, not transferable.
  Live count: ownerOf on 0x0C185ad21a28c62969711b8F57f1Ed3ceEB504d2 (Ethereum). Names: ham3d.fun/backers.
- Launching on Robinhood Chain after the audit. Six months, one release a month:
  1 daily run on mainnet, 2 Power-Up guns, 3 rivals and crews, 4 Royale beta, 5 seats, replays, buyback, 6 tournament and new rooms.
- HAM is an art project first ("Home's a Maze"); money is a mechanic. Tagline: Find your way home.
